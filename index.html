Qadam 1

Profil sahifasiga o'ting

Ilovani oching va pastki navigatsiyadan "Profil" bo'limiga o'ting. Profil sahifasida obuna ma'lumotlari va sozlamalar ko'rinadi. Sahifaning eng pastiga aylantiring.

02

"Hisobni o'chirish" tugmasini bosing

"Hisobni o'chirish" (qizil tugma) ni bosing. Tasdiqlash oynasi paydo bo'ladi. Oynada "Davom etish" tugmasini bosing.

03
Qadam 3

Tasdiqlang

Ikkinchi tasdiqlash oynasida "Ha, o'chirish" tugmasini bosing. Hisobingiz va barcha ma'lumotlaringiz butunlay o'chiriladi.

Account Deletion — English summary

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for the Chinora mobile application (com.chinora.app), published on Google Play by the developer umidjon_company.

In-app: open the app → Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → tap "Hisobni o'chirish" (Delete account) → confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app: send a deletion request to umidjongafforov844@gmail.com or @chinora_admin on Telegram, including the phone number or email used to register. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Deleted data: profile information (name, email, phone), saved favourite patterns, notification settings, subscription and in-app activity history — permanently removed within 30 days. Retained data: anonymised payment records required by accounting and tax law; these are not linked to your profile.
