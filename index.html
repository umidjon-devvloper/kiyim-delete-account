Introduction

By using the Chinora application you agree to the terms set out in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the app.

We process personal data in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Personal Data and with Google Play Developer Program Policies. We collect only the minimum data required to operate the service, and we never sell it.

Data we collect

The table below lists the categories of data the app collects, the purpose of each, and whether it is required or optional.

Data type Purpose Status
Name Creating your profile and addressing you in the app Required
Email address Sign-in, password recovery, service notifications Required
Phone number Account verification (SMS/OTP) and customer support Required
Saved favourite patterns Storing and syncing the patterns you select Optional
Subscription and payment status Managing access to paid features and billing Required for subscriptions
Push token and notification settings Delivering push notifications Optional
Technical data (device model, OS version, app version, crash reports) Ensuring app stability and fixing errors Automatic

Data we do NOT collect

  • Precise location (GPS coordinates)
  • Contacts, SMS messages or call history
  • Bank card numbers — payments are processed entirely by the payment provider and card details never reach our servers
  • Microphone or camera recordings

How we use your data

  • To create, verify and manage your account
  • To provide the core features of the app (saving and syncing patterns)
  • To manage subscriptions and payments
  • To provide technical support
  • To improve service quality, detect and fix errors
  • To comply with legal obligations (accounting and tax reporting)

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data is transmitted over encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS) and may be stored on these providers' servers. They process the data solely to provide their service to us.

Sharing and disclosure

Your personal data is disclosed to third parties only in the following cases:

  • When you have given explicit consent
  • To operate the service technically (the providers listed above)
  • When required by law, a court order or a request from a competent government authority
  • When necessary to prevent fraud, abuse or a security threat

Data retention and security

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Once the account is deleted, all personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Exception: payment and tax records (transaction date and amount) are retained as required by law, but they are anonymised and are not linked to your profile.

For security we use TLS encryption, password hashing and restricted server access. Even so, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Your rights

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data through the in-app profile settings
  • Right to erasure — permanently delete your account and all associated data (see section 8)
  • Right to withdraw consent — turn off notifications or cancel your subscription

For any request, write to umidjongafforov844@gmail.com. We respond within 7 business days.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. Follow the steps below.

⚠️

Warning: once your account is deleted, all of your data, favourites and profile information are removed permanently and cannot be recovered.

01
Step 1

Open the Profile tab

Launch the app and open the Profile tab from the bottom navigation bar. Scroll to the bottom of the page.

02
Step 2

Tap the Delete account button

Tap the red Delete account button (labelled "Hisobni o'chirish" in the app). A confirmation dialog appears — choose Continue.

03
Step 3

Confirm

In the second confirmation dialog tap Yes, delete. Your account is disabled immediately.

If you cannot use the app — for example you have uninstalled it or cannot sign in — contact us at either address below. Include the phone number or email address you registered with and state that you want your account deleted. Requests are completed within 7 business days.

Children's privacy

Chinora is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we become aware that such data has been collected, it is deleted immediately. Parents or guardians with concerns may contact us at any time.
