Umumiy qoidalar

Chinora ilovasidan foydalanish orqali siz ushbu maxfiylik siyosatida bayon etilgan shartlarga rozilik bildirasiz. Agar siz ushbu shartlar bilan rozi bo'lmasangiz, iltimos, ilovadan foydalanmang.

Biz sizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni O'zbekiston Respublikasining "Shaxsga doir ma'lumotlar to'g'risida"gi qonuni hamda Google Play dasturchi siyosati talablariga muvofiq qayta ishlaymiz. Biz zarur bo'lgan minimal hajmdagi ma'lumotni yig'amiz va uni hech qachon sotmaymiz.

Ma'lumotlarni uzatish va oshkor qilish

Shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringiz faqat quyidagi hollarda uchinchi tomonlarga oshkor qilinadi:

  • Siz aniq rozilik bergan bo'lsangiz

  • Firibgarlik, suiiste'mol yoki xavfsizlik tahdidining oldini olish zarur bo'lganda

Ma'lumotlarni saqlash muddati va xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlaringiz hisobingiz faol bo'lgan davrda saqlanadi. Hisob o'chirilgach, barcha shaxsiy ma'lumotlar 30 kun ichida serverlarimizdan butunlay o'chiriladi.

Istisno: to'lov va soliq hisobotlariga oid yozuvlar (to'lov sanasi va summasi) qonun talabiga ko'ra saqlanadi, ammo ular shaxsni aniqlab bo'lmaydigan shaklga keltiriladi va profilingiz bilan bog'lanmaydi.

Xavfsizlik uchun biz TLS shifrlash, parollarni xeshlash va serverga cheklangan kirish nazoratini qo'llaymiz. Shunga qaramay, internet orqali uzatishning 100% xavfsizligini kafolatlash mumkin emasligini eslatib o'tamiz.

Privacy Policy

English version · Chinora (com.chinara.chinora) by umidjon_company · Last updated: 25 July 2026

This is the official Privacy Policy for the Chinora mobile application (package com.chinara.chinora), published on Google Play by the developer umidjon_company. It explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and phone number — used to create and verify your account and to provide support.
  • App activity: saved favourite patterns, notification settings, subscription status and in-app activity history.
  • Technical data: device model, OS version, app version and crash reports — used for stability and debugging.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera data. Card numbers are handled entirely by the payment provider and never reach our servers.

How we use it

To create and manage your account, deliver the app's core features, manage subscriptions and payments, provide support, improve reliability, and comply with legal and tax obligations. We never sell personal data and never share it for advertising.

Third-party services

Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play Billing (all Google LLC). Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Retention and security

Data is retained while your account is active. After deletion, all personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Anonymised payment records required by accounting and tax law are retained but are not linked to your profile. We use TLS encryption, password hashing and restricted server access.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correct it, withdraw consent, or delete your account entirely. Requests are handled within 7 business days.

Account and data deletion

In-app: open the app → Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → tap "Hisobni o'chirish" (Delete account) → confirm twice.
Without the app: email umidjongafforov844@gmail.com or message @chinora_admin on Telegram, including the phone number or email used to register.

Children's privacy

Chinora is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn that we have, it is deleted immediately.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Questions: umidjongafforov844@gmail.com.
